Before we begin

This viewer uses your camera and screen recording to create training data for an AI drawing model. Here’s what we collect:

  • Camera feed — recorded as part of each drawing session video
  • Operator snapshot — a frame captured at recording start to identify who is at the workstation
  • Drawing strokes — coordinates, pressure, and timing
  • Session recordings — composite video uploaded to Modal for AI training

All data is stored locally and on Modal cloud infrastructure. You can request deletion at any time.
