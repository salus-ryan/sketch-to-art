Before we begin

This studio uses your camera and screen recording to create training data for an AI drawing model. Here's exactly what we collect:

  • Camera feed — recorded as part of each drawing session video
  • Operator snapshot — a single frame captured at the start of each recording to identify who is at the workstation
  • Drawing strokes — coordinates, pressure, and timing of everything you draw
  • Session recordings — composite video of your canvas + camera, uploaded to Modal for AI training
  • Labels — any text labels you provide for your drawings

All data is stored locally and on Modal cloud infrastructure. Recordings and snapshots are retained for model training. You can request deletion at any time.
